An off-grid system in every model, as standard.

You might find the perfect camp by a creek, hours from the nearest powered site, and not want to leave. You'll still want the 220L compressor fridge cold and the coffee going in the morning, and that's what the off-grid system in every Wonderland RV is there for.

The solar panels charge a lithium battery, and your tow vehicle tops it up on the drive in. An inverter turns that into 240V, so the power points work without plugging in, including the ones outside above the tunnel boot and the picnic table.

There's an Anderson plug for portable solar too, so you can park in the shade and still catch the sun.

Each model comes with its own system from Redarc or Victron, and on the Solara and XTR you can choose between two.
