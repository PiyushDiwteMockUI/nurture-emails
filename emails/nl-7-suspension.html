What sits underneath each Wonderland RV.

If you're heading for a camp at the end of a long dirt road, say the Gibb River Road, with corrugations most of the way in, you want to pull up with everything inside right where you packed it. A lot of that comes down to what's under the van.

The XTR, Solara and Hornet sit on Cruisemaster airbag suspension as standard, rated to a 4.5T load capacity. On the Amaroo you can choose from three coil systems, and one of them comes airbag ready.

On the airbag vans, the controls are in the outdoor pantry, right where you set up camp.
